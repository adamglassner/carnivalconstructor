An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
=============================================

Open Carnival Constructor from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your
desktop, or Ironite Launcher. Or just double-click CarnivalConstructor.exe.

THE DEMO
You run a traveling carnival. Play three nights at the Pine Hollow County
Fair, then tear down and hit the road. The full game takes you on tour.

HOW TO PLAY
- Daytime: pick a ride, midway game, food stand or extra from the bar at
  the bottom and click the lot to build it. Guests line up at the gold
  square in front of it. Click anything you've built to set its price,
  set a midway game's prize odds, or sell it.
- Night: press OPEN THE GATES. From 6 to 11 PM guests pay admission,
  ride, play, eat and spend. Space pauses; 1, 2 and 3 set the speed.
- Keep guests happy: rides for fun, food and drink for hunger and
  thirst, benches for tired feet, light poles for dark corners, and
  trash cans to keep the litter down.
- Fair or rigged: a rigged game pays out fewer prizes, but guests catch
  on, and a bad reputation means smaller crowds.
- Rides break down now and then. Click a broken ride and send a
  mechanic.
- Esc = cancel / menu, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen,
  Delete = sell what's selected.

Your settings and records are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\CarnivalConstructor,
outside the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All art, music and sounds are original and made by Ironite.
