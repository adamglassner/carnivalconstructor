# Changelog

All notable changes to Carnival Constructor Classic are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH). Versions before 1.0.0 are the free demo.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-11

### Changed
- The game is now called Carnival Constructor Classic: on the title screen, the top bar, the window, the installer and the Start menu. Your saved run, settings and achievements carry over.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- Nights go on forever. Keep your carnival running at Pine Hollow night after night instead of stopping after three. The top bar shows which night you're on.
- Footpaths. On the new Paths tab, click and drag across the lot to lay paths ($10 a tile), or take them up again for $5 back. Guests prefer to walk on paths, walk faster on them and stay happier; tramping across the grass wears it down and makes for muddy shoes on drizzly nights. Wandering guests stroll along your paths. Building over a path takes it up.
- Continue. Your run is saved every morning (and as you build), so you can quit and pick up where you left off with Continue on the title screen. New Game starts fresh.
- New achievements: Season Regular (ten nights), Bankroll ($25,000 in cash) and Paved Paradise (40 tiles of path). Opening Weekend and Honest Carny now unlock after three nights.

### Changed
- The title screen shows your longest run and best cash.

### Fixed
- The lights at night were far too bright: where light poles, rides and food stands overlapped, the lot washed out to a glare. Lights are now soft pools of warm light, so the night stays dark around them.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- The first playable demo of Carnival Constructor: three nights at the Pine Hollow County Fair.
- Build on a 24 x 14 fairground lot: the Carousel, Teacup Twirl and Ferris Wheel; Ring Toss, Balloon Darts and the Duck Pond; Corn Dogs, Funnel Cake and Lemon Shake-Ups; benches, trash cans and light poles. The Gravity Barrel, Wild Mouse and High Striker are shown as coming in the full game.
- Daytime building and pricing, then nights from 6 to 11 PM with pause and three speeds.
- Guests with names, money, hunger, thirst and energy who walk the lot, line up, ride, play, eat, drop litter, think out loud and go home happy (or not). Paths wear into the grass where they walk most.
- Ticket prices for every attraction and gate admission. Guests grumble about high prices.
- Prize odds for every midway game, from fair & square to rigged. Rigging pays out fewer prizes but guests catch on and your reputation drops.
- Reputation (out of five stars) that sets the size of the next night's crowd.
- Ride breakdowns and mechanics, darkness and light poles, drizzly nights, and litter cleanup costs.
- A results screen after every night and a summary when you tear down after the third.
- Nine achievements, which also show in Ironite Launcher.
- A calliope waltz and sound effects, all made by Ironite. Music toggles with M, full screen with F11.
- Settings and records saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\CarnivalConstructor.
